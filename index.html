Privacy Policy for BlueLock BlueLock values your privacy. 1. Data Collection: BlueLock does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal identity data, telemetry, or behavioral tracking to any external server. 2. Bluetooth & Proximity: The app only utilizes local Bluetooth capabilities to detect proximity signals (such as RSSI) from your paired devices solely for the purpose of triggering the lock screen locally. 3. Security: All configurations and device preferences remain strictly on your local machine. 4. Contact: If you have any questions, please contact the developer via the official repository or support email.
